# Code of conduct

Perch is a friendly place to report bugs, share ideas and send code. To keep it that way:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Plenty of people here write in their second or third language.
- Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or sexual or violent content.
- Stay on topic. No spam or self-promotion unrelated to Perch.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments and issues that break these rules, and block people who keep
breaking them. To report something privately, email idlebenchhq@gmail.com.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
