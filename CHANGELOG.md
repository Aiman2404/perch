# Changelog

Every version is also on the [releases page](https://github.com/idlebench/perch/releases) with an installer.

## 0.2.2 (2026-11-13)

- Perch moved to **github.com/idlebench/perch**. Update checks, homepage and links point to the new address.

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-13)

- **Glass look**: the whole island becomes frosted glass made from the album cover.
- New `perch.` wordmark logo and app icon.
- Fixed: pictures dragged from browsers, chat apps, Photos and screenshot previews can now be dropped on the Shelf.
- Fixed: theme previews all showed the selected theme.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-13)

- **Automatic, signed updates.**
- Fixed: the island could open black for a few seconds after hovering again quickly.
- Fixed: memory now falls back after closing the Perch window.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-13)

- First public build: now playing for any app, scroll for volume, Ctrl + scroll for brightness, Apps, Shelf,
  Clipboard, Sound and Quick tabs, live activities, the Perch window with themes.
